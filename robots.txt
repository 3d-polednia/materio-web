# The signed-in workspace and shared estimates stay out of the index, and the thing that
# keeps them out is the robots noindex meta tag on both pages — not a Disallow here.
#
# Until session 30 this file also said "Disallow: /app/" and "Disallow: /p/", which
# undid exactly that: a crawler told not to fetch a page never reads the noindex on it,
# and a URL it was told not to fetch can still be listed on the strength of a link
# somewhere else. For /p/<token> that is worse than an ordinary listing, because the
# token in the URL is the whole credential — the listing would publish it. Crawling
# them costs two pages and nothing is private on either: /app/ signed out is a sign-in
# form, and /p/ with no token renders no estimate.
