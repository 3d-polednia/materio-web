Cookies and browser storage

Exactly what the site keeps in your browser, what for, and how to change your mind at any time.

Your consent

Google Analytics is off by default and only starts once you accept on the banner. You can withdraw that as easily as you gave it.

Not decided yet

What the site itself stores

This data never leaves your browser. We do not send it anywhere and cannot reach it. Clearing the site data deletes it.

Name Kind What for How long
materio_consent localStorage Your decision about analytics. Without it the banner would ask on every visit. Until you clear the site data
materio-lang localStorage The language you picked in the switcher, so the next visit lands in it straight away. Until you clear the site data
liczmat-currency localStorage The currency your prices are counted in. Picked separately from the language. Until you clear the site data
liczmat-theme localStorage Light or dark, if you set it by hand instead of leaving it to the system. Until you clear the site data
liczmat-signed-in localStorage The account level (LiczMat or LiczMat Pro) remembered at sign-in. The calculators do not load Firebase but need this to decide whether to offer you an account, and to mark the header when somebody is signed in. It holds no e-mail address and nothing else about you, and it unlocks nothing. Until you sign out
liczmat-remember localStorage Whether you stay signed in after the browser closes. You set it when signing in or in the profile; the value applies to this device only. Until you clear the site data
materio-redirected sessionStorage A guard against a redirect loop when the language changes. Until the tab closes
materio-workspace-v1 localStorage Your projects, rooms and estimate lines saved without signing in. Until you clear the site data
materio-active-project localStorage Which project is active, so a new line goes into the right one. Until you clear the site data
liczmat-recent-calcs localStorage Which calculators were used last, so the dashboard can list them. The tool's name and a date only — no inputs and no results. Until you clear the site data
liczmat-crm-v1 localStorage LiczMat Pro clients, jobs and quotes: names, contact details, notes, statuses, dates, the projects filed under them, labour lines and the margin. Until you clear the site data

What Google's services store

Only when you use them: analytics after you accept, Firebase after you sign in, the Google map on the stores page.

Name Kind What for How long
_ga, _ga_* cookie Google Analytics: anonymous, aggregated site traffic. Written only after you accept. Up to 2 years
firebaseLocalStorageDb IndexedDB The sign-in session under “My account” and a local copy of the account data. Only once signed in. Until you sign out
google.com / maps.google.com cookie The Google map embedded on the stores page sets Google's own files when it loads. Per Google's policy
