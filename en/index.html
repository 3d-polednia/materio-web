Account and sync

A LiczMat account is optional. The calculator works without signing in — an account is only needed to save projects and rooms and have them on both your phone and your browser.

The account is optional

Without signing in everything is calculated on your device, exactly as before.

The same project on your phone and in your browser

Once you sign in, projects, rooms, estimates and shopping lists go to Google Firestore and come back on your other device.

Only you can see your data

The security rules let only the owner reach an account's data. The one exception is a link you create yourself.

The data sits in the European Union

The Firestore database runs in the europe-central2 (Warsaw) region.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is LiczMat free?

Yes. LiczMat is free to download and use. There is no subscription and no paid features. The account is optional and also free. The app is funded by Google AdMob ads.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. All the calculators and the cutting optimizer run offline, on your phone. Without an account your projects and results stay on the device. Cloud sync needs a sign-in and a connection.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You open the app and start calculating. You only create an account if you want your saved projects and rooms on both the phone and the browser.

How does store search work?

You type a city or store name and LiczMat shows the nearest places on Google Maps and guides you to the one you pick. You can also share your location to see stores around you.

What data does LiczMat collect?

Without an account your projects, estimates and settings are stored only on the device. Once you sign in, projects, rooms, estimates and shopping lists are copied to Google Firestore (europe-central2, Warsaw) where only your account can see them. The app uses Google services (AdMob ads and maps for the store finder) which process limited data. It is all described in the Privacy Policy.

Which languages and currencies does LiczMat work in?

The site is in Polish, Ukrainian, German and English. The currency is chosen separately from the language: PLN, EUR, USD or UAH. Nothing is converted at an exchange rate — the currency applies to the prices you type in yourself.

Which phones does LiczMat run on?

On phones and tablets running Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer.
