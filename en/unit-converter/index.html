How we calculate it

Every category has a base unit. The value goes into the base unit and from there into the target one, so any pair of units works the same way.

Temperature is the exception: the scales differ in their zero as well, so °C, °F and K go through degrees Celsius instead of a factor.

The site's engine is a 1:1 port of the Android app's code.

What it converts

  • Length — mm, cm, dm, m, km, in, ft, yd, mi, nmi
  • Area — mm², cm², dm², m², a, ha, km², in², ft², yd², ac, mi²
  • Volume — ml, cl, l, m³, cm³, dm³, gal, qt, pt, fl oz, ft³
  • Mass — mg, g, dag, kg, t, oz, lb, st
  • Temperature — °C, °F, K
  • Speed — m/s, km/h, mph, kn, ft/s
  • Time — ms, s, min, h, d, wk
  • Pressure — Pa, hPa, kPa, bar, mbar, atm, psi, mmHg
  • Energy — J, kJ, cal, kcal, Wh, kWh
  • Power — W, kW, MW, hp, KM
  • Data — B, kB, MB, GB, TB, KiB, MiB, GiB
