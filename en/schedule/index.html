              The schedule stores nothing of its own. A deadline is a field of the job, so a date changed here is the same date you see under “Jobs”.

              The jobs and their deadlines are kept in this browser's storage (localStorage). They are not sent anywhere and they are not in the Android app — the sync covers projects, rooms, calculations and material lists. Clearing site data deletes the jobs along with their deadlines.
