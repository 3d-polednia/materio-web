What else LiczMat Pro covers

  • Clients — A list of clients with contact details, notes and the history of working with them.
  • Jobs — A job with its client, description, status, date, value and the project it belongs to.
  • Quotes — Material, labour, other costs, margin and the total, from what you have already counted.
  • History and CRM — The path through the whole job: client → job → project → quote → history.
