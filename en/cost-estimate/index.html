The estimate is made of the lines saved with the “Add to the estimate” button under a calculator result.

How to build an estimate in LiczMat

  1. Create a project under Projects and make it active.
  2. Calculate a material, enter its price and press “Add to the estimate”.
  3. Come back here and print the estimate or save it as a PDF.
