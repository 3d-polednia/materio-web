Cutting a furniture board with less waste

How many boards to buy for a list of parts, and how to lay them out so the off-cut is as small as possible. The saw's kerf takes material too.

Step by step

  1. List the parts: width, length and quantity. Add an allowance for edging if the edge band goes on after cutting.
  2. Give the board format and the saw kerf. A panel saw takes about 3–4 mm on every cut; over a few dozen cuts that is whole centimetres.
  3. Decide whether parts may be rotated. A board with a grain or a pattern cannot be, and the waste grows — calculate both and compare.

Tip

The result is a cutting plan, not an order. Before you order, check that every part fits the way the algorithm turned it — with a grain, rotating a part changes how the front looks even when the dimensions are right.
