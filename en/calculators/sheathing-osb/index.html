How we calculate it

What you enter

  • Area (m²)
  • Sheet width (mm)
  • Sheet length (mm)
  • Waste (%)

What to watch out for

The result is a count of whole sheets with no cutting plan. If you need to know how to cut them, use the 2D sheet cutting calculator.

Formula

sheet area = (Sheet width ÷ 1000) × (Sheet length ÷ 1000)
with allowance = Area × (1 + Waste ÷ 100)
sheets = ⌈ with allowance ÷ sheet area ⌉

The LiczMat app for Android uses the same formula. The site's engine is a 1:1 port of the app's code.
