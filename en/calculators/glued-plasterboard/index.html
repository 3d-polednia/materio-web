How we calculate it

What you enter

  • Area (m²)
  • Adhesive (kg/m²)

Formula

boards = ⌈ Area × 1.10 ÷ 2.4 ⌉
gypsum adhesive = ⌈ Area × Adhesive ÷ 25 ⌉ bags

What to watch out for

The adhesive-dab method only works on a flat, sound substrate. Bigger deviations need a metal frame.
