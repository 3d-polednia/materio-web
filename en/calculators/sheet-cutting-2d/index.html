How we calculate it

What you enter

  • Sheet width (mm)
  • Sheet length (mm)
  • Saw kerf (mm)
  • Pieces: w×l×qty (mm)
  • Rotate pieces

What to watch out for

Guillotine cutting: every cut runs right across the sheet, the way a panel saw works. Turn rotation off when the sheet has a grain or a directional pattern.

Formula

1. Expand the list into individual parts and sort them by area, largest first.
2. Each part lands in the free rectangle with the smallest leftover (best-area-fit),
   rotated by 90° if Rotate pieces is on.
3. A guillotine cut splits the used rectangle into a right and a bottom offcut,
   each shrunk by Saw kerf.
4. When a part fits nowhere, a new sheet is opened.
actual waste = (boards × Sheet width × Sheet length − Σ parts) ÷ (boards × Sheet width × Sheet length) × 100%

The site's engine is a 1:1 port of the Android app's code.

Frequently asked questions

How many sheets will my parts take?

The calculator places the parts largest first into the free rectangle with the smallest leftover. When nothing fits any more, it opens another sheet, and that is what you buy.

When should you turn rotation off?

When the board has a grain or a directional pattern. A rotated part would then run visibly the wrong way even though the size fits.
